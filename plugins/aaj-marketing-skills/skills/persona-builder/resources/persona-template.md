# Persona & ICP template

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- **Segment / fit:** [B2B: industry, company size, model, tech; B2C: situation/need state]
- **Trigger to fit:** [what makes a company/person a fit *now*]
- **Disqualifiers:** [who looks like a fit but isn't]
- **Why we win here:** [the fit between their need and the product's strength]

## Persona: [name / role label]
- **Role & context:** [title, team, what their day looks like]
- **Primary goal:** [what they're trying to achieve — the outcome they're measured on]
- **Pains / frustrations:** [what's blocking that goal today] — *(evidence: [quote/source] or HYPOTHESIS)*
- **Buying trigger:** [the event that starts a search] — *(evidence or HYPOTHESIS)*
- **Decision criteria:** [what they compare on]
- **Objections:** [top 2–3 reasons they'd hesitate or say no]
- **Where they research:** [channels, communities, sources they trust]
- **Role in purchase:** [decision-maker | user | influencer | blocker]
- **Message that matters:** [the one thing to say to this persona]
- **Reach them via:** [the channels that fit, feeding paid-media / content choices]

(Repeat for each persona — aim for 1–3 real buyers, not a roster.)
