=== LANDING PAGE COPY ===
Roughly 150 words, in my first-person founder voice.

Say what the kit covers and who it is for: a founder who has results and no published proof,
because nobody asked the customer at the time and asking now feels awkward. The idea that makes this
one different: every case study has two separate questions - does it convince a sceptic, and may you
publish it - and most teams tangle them together, which is why both stall. The kit keeps them apart
and puts each step where it belongs in time, starting with a clause at kickoff.

"What's inside" - the ten sections, in order: the kickoff clause; the baseline record; the results
interview; the evidence worksheet; the permission request; the consent record; the write-up; the
anonymised version; the check before it goes live; running it with your agent.

FAQ - use these four:
  Q: What is the difference between a testimonial and a case study?
  A: A case study has a measured before and after. Without a baseline, what you have is a
     testimonial - a different asset with a different job, not a weaker case study. The kit tells
     you which one you have before you write anything.
  Q: What if the customer will not let us name them?
  A: Publish an anonymised version with the real numbers. A specific descriptor - size, what they
     do, and where - carries most of the credibility of a name and needs far less to approve. The
     kit shows how to build one.
  Q: Is the kickoff clause legal advice?
  A: No. It is a starting point for your contract. Have your own counsel review it.
  Q: Is the template free, and what format is it?
  A: Free, in exchange for an email address. It is a Word .docx you can type into directly, and it
     opens editable in Word, Google Docs, Pages and LibreOffice.
