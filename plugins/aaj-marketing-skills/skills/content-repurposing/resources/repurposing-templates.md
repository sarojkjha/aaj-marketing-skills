# Repurposing templates

Fill-in skeletons for turning one pillar into a distribution set. Copy a block, replace the
`[bracketed]` prompts. **Every stat, quote, and claim must come from the pillar or a source it
cites — never invent one to make a piece punchier.**

---

## 0 · Unit inventory (do this first)

| Unit | From the pillar | Source (if a stat/quote) | Best formats |
|------|-----------------|--------------------------|--------------|
| Core thesis (1 sentence) | [thesis] | — | carousel, thread, newsletter |
| Stat 1 | [number + what it means] | [named source] | stat card, hook post |
| Stat 2 | [number + what it means] | [named source] | stat card, thread beat |
| Strong line / quote | [verbatim line] | [pillar] | quote graphic, post |
| Framework / list | [steps or items] | — | carousel, thread, checklist |
| Contrarian claim | [the "actually…" point] | — | hook post, thread opener |

---

## 1 · LinkedIn carousel (PDF/doc, 6–10 slides)

- **Slide 1 — Hook:** [boldest stat OR contrarian claim, large]. No preamble.
- **Slide 2 — Context/stakes:** [why this matters to the reader now]
- **Slides 3–N — One point each:** [stat / step / myth-vs-truth], skimmable, one idea per slide
- **Penultimate — Takeaway:** [the synthesis — what to do with this]
- **Final — CTA:** [read the full pillar → link] / [book a call]

Rollout: export as PDF, upload **natively** as a document. Re-upload to the personal profile
rather than resharing the company post; leave **24–48h** between company and personal.

---

## 2 · X / Twitter thread

```
1/ [hook — a bold number or claim from the pillar. No link.]
2/ [point 1 + its data/example]
3/ [point 2 + its data/example]
4/ [point 3 …]
5/ [takeaway] → full breakdown: [link to pillar]
```
For newer accounts, threads tend to outperform carousels on X.

---

## 3 · LinkedIn text post

```
[Line 1 — hook that lands before the "…more" fold]
[Line 2 — sharpen or reframe it]

[One insight. Short lines. Whitespace.]
[Optional: 2–3 line micro-list.]

[Question or soft CTA.]
```
Consider putting the link in the first comment rather than the body.

---

## 4 · Newsletter blurb

- **Subject / preview:** [the hook — a number or a promise]
- **Body:** [the one insight in 2–4 sentences] + [why it matters to the reader]
- **CTA:** [read the pillar → link]

---

## 5 · Stat card (single image)

- **Number:** [the striking figure]
- **Point of view:** [one opinionated line — the "so what"]
- **Source:** [named source] · [site/handle]

---

## 6 · Short-form video / reel (script)

```
0–3s  HOOK: [number/claim on screen + spoken]
3–20s INSIGHT: [the one point, plainly]
20s   CTA: [what to do next]
```

---

## 7 · Quote graphic

- **Line:** [one strong sentence from the pillar]
- **Attribution:** [author / brand]

## 8 · Sequencing calendar (example — space over ~2 weeks)

| Day | Channel | Piece | Links to pillar |
|-----|---------|-------|-----------------|
| 1 | Pillar | Publish [title] | — |
| 2 | X | Thread | yes |
| 3 | LinkedIn (company) | Carousel (native doc) | yes |
| 5 | LinkedIn (personal) | Re-upload carousel | yes |
| 6 | LinkedIn/X | Stat card #1 | yes |
| 8 | LinkedIn | Text post | in comment |
| 10 | Newsletter | Blurb | yes |
| 12 | LinkedIn/X | Stat card #2 / quote graphic | yes |

Lead with the strongest hook; hold some units for a second wave. Every piece links back to the
pillar so the canonical page keeps accruing traffic and authority.
