- **Channel & timing:** [email, in-app, post-onboarding…]
- **Target length:** [≤ 5–8 questions for most; state est. minutes]

## Question types & when to use
- **Open-ended** — discovery, "why", unmet needs. (Limit to 1–3; they cost completion.)
- **Single-select / multiple-choice** — known categories, mutually exclusive options.
- **Likert scale (1–5 or 1–7)** — agreement/satisfaction; keep scale direction consistent.
- **NPS (0–10)** — loyalty; always pair with an open "why".
- **PMF (Sean Ellis)** — "How would you feel if you could no longer use [product]?" (Very/Somewhat/Not disappointed) + "why".
- **Ranking** — relative priority among a few options (use sparingly).

## Structure (order)
1. **Screener** (if needed) — qualify the respondent.
2. **Easy engagement question** — low effort, builds momentum.
3. **Core measurement** — the rating/scale questions tied to your decisions.
4. **The "why"** — open-ended follow-up to the core rating.
5. **Topic groups** — keep related questions together.
6. **Sensitive / demographic** — last, optional where possible.
7. **Thank-you + optional follow-up opt-in.**

## Wording checklist
- [ ] Neutral, not leading or loaded.
- [ ] One idea per question (no "fast and easy").
- [ ] Balanced options (equal positive/negative; neutral where apt).
- [ ] Plain language; no internal jargon.
- [ ] Consistent scale direction throughout.
- [ ] Piloted with 3–5 people before fielding.
